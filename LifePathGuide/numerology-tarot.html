A Balanced Approach

Why Combine Numerology and Tarot?

Numerology provides our framework for broader themes and personal cycles, highlighting the natural rhythms and developmental seasons of your life. Tarot offers a complementary way to explore your current situation, immediate possibilities, and real choices in real time.

Neither system is treated as a rigid predictive script. Instead, they serve as reflective mirrors that leave full room for interpretation, personal values, and your own free choices.

No prior knowledge is needed at all. Simon explains every concept in clear, everyday language, focusing on how it relates directly to your life.

You will be asked for your full date of birth and birth name. This information is used strictly to prepare your numerology calculations prior to our call, ensuring our full 60 minutes can be spent in conversation.

No. Consultations are designed for self-reflection, perspective, and informed decision-making. Particular outcomes or absolute predictions cannot be guaranteed; your choices and actions remain entirely your own.
