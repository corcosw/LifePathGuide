The Process

What to Expect During Your Session

Hypnotherapy is a collaborative state of focused, calm awareness. You remain fully conscious and in control throughout.

Rather than making dramatic claims about "dissolving" or "rewiring" everything overnight, my approach is measured and realistic. Change is a process: we identify the specific situations where you feel stuck, use guided relaxation and suggestion to help your mind respond with greater calm, and review simple daily tools you can use between sessions.

While many clients experience noticeable shifts after an initial session, lasting behavioural change typically benefits from consolidation. We always review your progress together so you can decide what further support, if any, is right for you.

1

Discuss your goals

We spend the first 15 minutes discussing what you want to change, reviewing your current triggers, and checking suitability for hypnotherapy.

2

The guided hypnotherapy session

Around 25–30 minutes of gentle, guided relaxation. Simon introduces positive, constructive suggestions focused strictly on your agreed goals.

3

Review and next steps

In the final 10 minutes, we review how the experience felt, agree on a simple daily grounding practice, and discuss whether further sessions are needed.

Service Boundaries & FAQs

Practical Questions & Important Boundaries

Clear, honest answers regarding what hypnotherapy involves and when other forms of care are advised.

? Will I remain aware and in control during the session?

Yes, absolutely. Hypnosis is not sleep or unconsciousness; it is a relaxed state of focused attention. You will hear everything said, remember the experience, and can speak or open your eyes at any moment you choose.

? What if I find it difficult to relax or switch off my mind?

Many people worry about this, especially if they experience racing thoughts. Hypnotherapy does not require you to "empty your mind" or have any prior meditation experience. Simon guides the pacing gently to suit you.

? How do Zoom appointments work in practice?

You will need a quiet room where you won't be interrupted for an hour, a comfortable armchair or sofa where your head and neck are supported, and a stable internet connection. Headphones or earbuds are recommended for optimal audio clarity.

? What are the confidentiality and privacy arrangements?

All information shared before, during, and after our appointments is treated with strict professional confidentiality. The only exceptions are legal disclosures required by UK law (e.g. risk of serious harm to yourself or others), which Simon will discuss transparently if applicable.

? When is hypnotherapy NOT appropriate?

Hypnotherapy is a supportive modality and is not a substitute for clinical psychiatric treatment, psychotherapy, or medical care. It is not suitable for individuals currently experiencing active psychosis, clinical depression without medical supervision, or severe personality disorders. During your initial inquiry or free 15-minute call, Simon will honestly assess suitability and advise if medical or specialist care should be consulted first.

? What happens after I submit an enquiry?

Simon personally reads your enquiry and replies within 24 hours. If you requested a free 15-minute intro call or a full session, he will suggest several date/time options and provide a short pre-consultation questionnaire. Appointments are only confirmed once both sides have agreed.
