My Journey & Core Philosophy

For over a decade, I have explored the meeting place between classical numerology systems, symbolic tarot archetypes, and conversational hypnotherapy. Having seen how often esoteric systems are weighed down with confusing jargon or fatalistic predictions, I set out to provide grounded, thoughtful consultations that respect your intelligence and individual agency.

A consultation is not about being told what you "must" do; it is an open, focused dialogue designed to help you step out of everyday reaction mode and look at your options with fresh eyes.
