Your Practitioner

Meet Simon Corcoran

I work with individuals who are navigating transitions, seeking fresh perspective, or looking to understand their personal cycles with greater clarity.

My background brings together over a decade of studying classical Pythagorean numerology, biorhythm cycles, symbolic tarot archetypes, and clinical hypnotherapy training. Rather than treating these tools as rigid fortune-telling, I approach every session and report as a grounded dialogue where your questions and personal agency remain central.

Based in Wokingham, Berkshire (UK), I offer live face-to-face consultations locally and globally via Zoom, offering a quiet, respectful space where you can speak candidly about what matters to you.
