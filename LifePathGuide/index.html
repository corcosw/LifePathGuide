1. The Life Path Calculation Method

Reduce each unit of your birth date (Day, Month, Year) separately to a single digit by adding its numbers together. Preserve Master Numbers 11, 22, and 33 without reducing. Then sum all three reduced values together, reducing once more if necessary.
